    Roadmap to the Platinum

    The platinum, Vice Got a Price!, unlocks when the other 54 trophies are earned. Follow these stages in order and you will never have to backtrack.

    1. Stage 1 — Play the story (Chapters 1–4)

      Play every story mission in order. This earns the chapter trophies Paradise Lost, T**s Ass Drug Money, Vice City, Baby! and Top Of The World, Ma, the mission trophies Trust, Break the Bank, Family Matters, DWNFALL, Hi-Jacked and Snow Storm, the opening trophy Just Like Paradise and the finale gold You Gotta Know When to Get Off. Do the missable moments as they come — see the Missable view — because story moments do not always come back.

    2. Stage 2 — Strangers and side missions

      Finish the stranger strands: Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone each have a final-mission trophy, and any 8 complete stranger strands earn the silver Strangers & Freaks. Hunt the unique animal for Bret (Fair Game) while you are out exploring.

    3. Stage 3 — Activities and challenges

      Sample all 9 activity types once (Master of None), gold all 42 Shooting Range challenges (The Shootist), win drone races at all 14 Top Fun locations (Top Fun), win 20 different races (Earn Cash, Not Kudos), beat Cal at fishing (Baited My Tackle), finish one arcade game (Stick Fiddler) and a bowling game (Let’s Go Bowling), and complete 50 challenges (One Last Thing). Work on the 250 stunts (Cunning Stunts) whenever you drive.

    4. Stage 4 — Collectibles and exploration

      Meditate at all 18 spots (Positive Mental Attitude), complete 3 card sets (Don’t Touch My Deck), WAiNK It at 100 locations (You WAiNKer!), discover 100 vehicles (Whip It Good), explore all 52 areas of Leonida (The Goodtime State) and buy all 8 garage properties (Little Boxes).

    5. Stage 5 — Crime, cash and clean-up

      Rob 5 bank vaults (Maximum Withdrawal), an armored truck (A Classic Blitz Play), one illegal operation per gang — 9 gangs (Pest Control) — and a jewelry store undetected (Sneakthief). Launder $250,000 of marked bills (Dime on the Dollar), spend $100,000 in shops (Treat Yo Self), register 10 vehicles in the Scooter Bros app (On the Spot), and drop 5 enemies in one Combat Focus (Gunslinger).

    6. Stage 6 — 100% completion

      When every category above is done, check the in-game completion figure and mop up whatever is short for the gold A Long Road (100% completion). The platinum Vice Got a Price! pops with it.

    Counts on this roadmap come straight from the official trophy descriptions: 4 chapters, 42 shooting-range challenges, 14 drone-race locations, 20 races, 9 activity types, 8 stranger strands, 18 meditation spots, 100 WAiNK locations, 100 vehicles, 52 areas, 8 garages, 5 bank vaults, 9 gangs, 50 challenges, 250 stunts, $250,000 laundered and $100,000 spent.

    Missable Trophies — Do These First

    These trophies are tied to specific moments, encounters or conditions that may not come back. Handle them the first time the chance appears; everything else on the list can be cleaned up in free roam after the story.
